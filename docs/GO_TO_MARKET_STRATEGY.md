- "AI-powered trend intelligence" → Generic, 100 competitors
- "Social listening for enterprises" → You're NOT Brandwatch
- "Disrupting the $5B social analytics market" → VCs hate this

### ❌ Don't Target This:
- Fortune 500 CMOs → They want Brandwatch (compliance, audit trails, official APIs)
- Data analysts → They want raw data, not dashboards

### ❌ Don't Launch Like This:
- Paid ads before PMF → Waste of money
- Press release → No journalist cares about "AI trend tool #473"

- "Stealth mode" → Opposite problem, but equally bad

### ❌ Don't Build This Next:
- White-label enterprise version → Kills your speed moat
- Mobile app → Premature (web works fine)
- Integrations (Slack, Zapier) → Solve distribution first
- Freemium tiers → Define pricing after PMF

## Pricing Strategy (Post-PMF)

**Don't mention pricing in beta.** But have this ready for when users ask:

### Proposed Tiers (After 50+ Daily Actives)

| Tier | Price | Target | Limits |
|------|-------|--------|--------|
| **Free** | $0/mo | Individuals testing | 10 lookups/day, 7-day history |
| **Starter** | $49/mo | Performance marketers | 100 lookups/day, 30-day history, CSV export |
| **Growth** | $199/mo | Growth teams (3-5 people) | Unlimited lookups, 90-day history, API access |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Agencies (5+ campaigns) | White-label, SLA, dedicated support |

**Key decisions**:
- Start at $49/mo (not $9.99 → signals "toy")
- Usage-based (lookups), not seat-based (easier to explain)
- Free tier to capture inbound (convert 5-10% to Starter)

**Do NOT launch paid tiers before 50+ daily actives.** Premature pricing kills momentum.
